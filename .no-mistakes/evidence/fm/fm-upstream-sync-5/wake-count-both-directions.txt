Wake-count fix (upstream 8b21c99) - both directions still fail for the right reason
=====================================================================

The fix is test-only: the four wake counts in
tests/fm-watch-triage.test.sh (exited-declared-pause case) now read a
.wake-queue that may legitimately not exist. To show both directions, the
exact assertion block from the test file (lines 1044-1050) is executed
against controlled queue states, in the merged ("fixed") form and with the
fix textually removed ("pre-fix") as a control.

      [ "$wakes" -le 1 ] || fail "dead-agent declared pause flooded $wakes stale wakes across six unchanged polls"
